# Prospective analysis plan

These choices were communicated to all three agents before the Monte Carlo timing pilot. The pilot is for implementation/timing checks, not selecting favorable DGPs, thresholds, profiles or reported sites. Scientific qualification does not block numerical execution. No real target outcomes may be opened or used in this study.

## Questions and estimands

The main question is when a Gaussian approximation to a scalar standardized Bernoulli risk difference is adequate for the fixed-menu design exercise. Distinguish existence/normality of the finite logistic parameter MLE from accuracy of the extended probability-MLE risk-difference functional. Distinguish exact Common-CATE A from structurally misspecified site-heterogeneous B. Neither simulated participants nor successful simulations establish additional real data or empirical scientific qualification.

All 57 original sites, 7,228 responses and the source/target split remain fixed in the design inventory. Only the 4,365 real source outcomes enter DGP fitting. Each simulation condition specifies a continuous population profile p_s inside the original observed-profile set. Unknown real labels remain unknown; the representative profile is a simulation assumption. The actual retained records are not relabeled, dropped or augmented.

At scale c in {1,2,5,10,20}, fix n_st(c)=c times the historical treatment-arm count. Hamilton largest-remainder apportionment allocates each arm into the three categories using n_st(c)p_s, with category-index ties. This preserves exact arm totals, treatment ratios, relative site sizes and the fixed population standardization weights. Realized integer category fractions approximate p_s, with recorded rounding discrepancies. It is a declared future stratified experimental design, not exact multiplication of historical within-arm category counts, which would preserve empty active cells forever.

## DGP calibration and sensitivity

All real source outcomes enter a category-cell Bernoulli likelihood. Known labels use their recorded cell; unknown labels use a prespecified uniform mixture over the three possible latent memberships. This is a working missing-membership assumption, not an empirical identification result. Raw cell-probability MLEs supply the frozen source-planning noise model. DGP probabilities maximize that likelihood with half-success/half-failure terms .5 sum[log(q)+log(1-q)] for generation only. Do not describe this penalty as the Jeffreys posterior mode. Finite simulated MLEs are not smoothed or repaired with these generating probabilities.

A uses these shared generating probabilities. B keeps control probabilities fixed and adds a prespecified centered bounded site perturbation to treatment probabilities, with h<=.05 chosen to avoid clipping. Its unweighted site perturbation mean is zero, which does not imply zero target-weighted or category-weighted bias. The exact site effects and pooled source moments are available from the simulation DGP and used for evaluation only.

Main conditions: A and B, barycenter profiles, all five scales, 5,000 evaluation replications each. Secondary conditions: three extreme feasible profile completions, and all nine pairs (q0Other,q1Other) in {.2,.5,.8}², under A at scales {1,5,20}, 2,000 evaluation replications each. There are 46 total conditions. Keep the raw source-planning probabilities fixed across these perturbations; the oracle planning model uses each condition's actual probabilities. This compares a fixed source estimate with an oracle, not repeated real-source-calibration uncertainty.

## Fixed menu and initial source model

The 12 candidate identities are selected before simulated outcomes: require positive planned counts in every positive-weight category/arm at all five barycenter scales, then deterministic farthest-first selection of distinct barycenter profiles, starting at the lexicographically first eligible site and breaking distance ties lexicographically. Report all 29 eligible sites versus all 57 diagnostic sites. This deliberate design-support constraint limits the bridge comparison's domain; it is not a claim that all sites support the approximation. The same 12 identities are retained in sensitivities. If a sensitivity creates an unidentified candidate functional, retain all site diagnostics, write unavailable bridge-policy rows for that condition, and continue other conditions; do not shrink the menu or invent the missing estimate.

Every replication regenerates all 40 source experiments, pools simulated category counts into three source contrasts, and draws future bridge responses independently even when their design identity is also a source. Pooling retains every simulated source participant. The initial source observation is three scalar coordinate measurements z_c=e_c'gamma+noise under A, not a claim that all 40 site MLEs succeed. The working base prior mean is zero and covariance I_3, fixed in advance. The source V0 and mean follow the declared Gaussian update. This is a local working prior; gamma in the Bernoulli DGP is fixed, not actually drawn from an unbounded Gaussian prior.

Under B, compute the true pooled source contrast mean and variance using each site's probabilities and allocation counts. A common mean is then misspecified. Oracle noise does not remove that mean misspecification. Report source design rank/singular values, posterior eigenvalues and condition numbers rather than concealing weak support with the declared prior.

## Estimators and failure accounting

For positive n and 0<K<n, the finite saturated logistic MLE is log(K/(n-K)), with qhat=K/n. At K=0 or n there is no finite logit MLE: flag separation, while separately reporting the mathematically defined extended probability MLE on [0,1]. An empty cell with p>0 makes the site functional unidentified, with no imputation. Empty p=0 cells are structurally irrelevant to that functional; report their count separately from active emptiness. Analytic closed-form fitting has no iterative solver nonconvergence; distinguish that fact from analytic MLE nonexistence.

Report all 57 sites, unconditional extended-estimator and finite-MLE availability, zero/invalid SE rates, and separately labeled conditional summaries. Unavailable intervals count as failures in unconditional coverage. Do not infer unconditional performance from only finite-MLE realizations. Oracle variance is exactly the variance of the defined extended probability-RD estimator; only its distributional Gaussian approximation is asymptotic. The uncorrected plug-in variance has a known finite-cell downward bias; retain it as requested, without post-hoc repair.

## Frozen adequacy criteria

For each site and variance standardization, report bias, variance ratios, skewness, excess kurtosis, quantiles and Q-Q discrepancies at probabilities {.025,.05,.25,.5,.75,.95,.975}, and 90%/95% Wald coverage. A functional adequacy flag requires: valid standardization in at least .99 of all replications; absolute mean standardized error <=.10; empirical-to-reference variance ratio in [.90,1.10]; absolute skewness <=.30; absolute excess kurtosis <=.50; maximum absolute Q-Q error <=.20 standard deviations; and both unconditional coverages within .015 of nominal. A separate strict-logit flag additionally requires finite-logit success >=.99. These are operational finite-grid criteria, not a theorem or a guarantee that all future settings satisfy them.

An all-site conclusion requires every one of the 57 sites to pass. A menu conclusion requires every one of its 12 designs to pass and the joint 12-vector to have validity >=.99, independent-normal rectangle coverage within .02 of nominal and maximum absolute off-diagonal standardized correlation <=.05. Report the passing fractions and each failure reason even when the global gate fails. Show Monte Carlo uncertainty; crossing a threshold is descriptive, not a multiple-testing-adjusted assertion of model truth. Report smallest observed qualifying scale and distinguish any non-monotonic finite-grid pass from persistent larger-scale adequacy.

## Bridge policies, simulation and oracle

For B={1,2,4}, enumerate all 12,66,495 subsets. Compare logdet greedy, exhaustive logdet, mean-target-variance greedy, exact uniform random subset averaging, mean-nearest-profile selection, exhaustive analytic Bernoulli MSE oracle and an independent-pilot Monte Carlo exhaustive oracle. The latter is selected from 2,000 independent pilot replications per condition, using mean squared error across all 17 target functionals, then scored on the fresh main evaluation stream. All methods share the same prior, decoder, profiles, budgets and evaluation draws. Candidate budgets count experiments; participant totals are reported separately.

The target-variance and nearest-profile objectives average over all 17 targets and choose one common subset per budget. The MC and analytic oracles are simulation-truth evaluation benchmarks, not available real-world policies. Theorem 5.6's approximation ratio belongs to logdet greedy only. Report exact information ratio, design agreement and target risk regret with paired Monte Carlo errors. A signed evaluation excess over the pilot-selected MC oracle can be negative; do not truncate it.

Report each target's MSE, MAE and nominal working-posterior interval coverage, along with averages. Repeated source noise and source shrinkage bias enter the decoder's exact sampling covariance and risk. Compute moment-matched Gaussian MSE, MAE and interval coverage from those same first two moments; compare with actual Bernoulli evaluation. MSE equality for a fixed affine decoder follows exactly from first two moments and is not evidence of Gaussian tails. A and B diagnostics centered at each site's true effect separate distributional approximation from a false common mean. Include the source-only/no-new-bridge baseline.
