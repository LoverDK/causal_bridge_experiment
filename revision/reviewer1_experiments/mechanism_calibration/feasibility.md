The local framing header includes source/country/language/WEIRD, subject pool, setting, tablet/pencil, execution and ordering fields, alongside participant answers. The official repository points to a participating-lab source-information sheet and a primary-analysis codebook. The sheet includes uncertain entries such as unverified online devices. The local source hash and commit are documented in `DATA_SOURCES.md`. Fifty-seven framing sources are replications of one task across contexts, not 57 different interventions with known mechanisms. Repeated slates, sources sharing `Source.Global`, and country/language dependence require a hierarchy, not arbitrary row independence. The original-effect summary contains noisy standardized outcome estimates; a common Cohen's d scale does not establish a common estimand across distinct tasks. Outcome answers (`tver*`), effect estimates and effect-family residuals must never be reclassified as mechanism labels. WEIRD is a coarse descriptor, not a validated mechanistic variable.

Missing for a mechanism claim: a science-defined coordinate dictionary, independent reference measurements with measurement error, complete-menu calibration units, deployment comparability, domain/scaling, omitted-factor policy, and L/H justification. A careful descriptor study can support conditional context sensitivity or a noisy-reference prediction audit, with its own assumptions.

## Missing-data and reference-coding plan

For every study and candidate coordinate record: stable study/arm/wave ID, construct definition, unit, time known, quoted source locator, allowed values, observed value or bounds, measurement error, coder confidence, provenance version, exposure status and category (design, baseline proxy, implementation/mediator, outcome). Retain explicit `unknown`, `not_reported`, `not_applicable`, `conflicting_sources` and `post_outcome_only`; never recode them as zero or absence.

Freeze the mapping and inclusion strata before coding. For a missing coordinate use a justified external domain interval, and propagate it into the mechanism set. If no domain/scale is supportable, mark qualification failure rather than inventing a finite coordinate or L/H. Keep nominal outcome-blind planned features separate from retrospectively measured implementation features. A text embedding or expert consensus can define a reference construct but does not identify an unobserved causal mechanism or certify omitted factors.

Use two independently working domain-competent human coders with target numeric effects and result summaries removed; preserve signed raw sheets. Pilot on three design-only records, revise the dictionary once, then freeze it before a fresh complete pass. A prespecified third adjudicator resolves disagreements after raw agreement is computed. Record accidental exposures, timing and residual bias; do not describe this retrospectively viewed archive as prospectively confirmatory.

Reliability: report per-coordinate exact agreement and missingness before adjudication; confusion counts and Cohen kappa for nominal fields, weighted kappa for prespecified ordinal fields; absolute agreement/limits for continuous fields plus an appropriate absolute-agreement ICC when units and variation justify it. Also report unknown/unknown agreement separately and agreement conditional on both being observed. Compute intervals with study-cluster resampling if a sufficiently large study sample exists. With seven studies, publish counts and wide uncertainty and avoid precise reliability claims; multiple fields or repeated waves are not independent studies. Adjudication consistency is not independent accuracy.

Planning costs, not observed costs: a seven-study design pilot at 1–3 hours/study/coder requires 14–42 coder-hours, plus 4–12 hours for dictionary/redaction and 4–10 hours of adjudication/source checking: approximately 22–64 person-hours before expert review. Monetary cost is this labor multiplied by actual agreed rates; no price is assumed. Obtaining independent reference measurements and complete calibration archives is unpriced and may dominate. A finite .95 joint-max rank first requires 19 *complete exchangeable audit archives*, not 19 labels. A seven-study pool cannot supply this by bootstrapping. More records would also be needed for separate development/target splits and a credible reliability estimate.
