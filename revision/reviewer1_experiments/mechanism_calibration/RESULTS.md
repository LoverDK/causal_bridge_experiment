# Incremental mechanism calibration stress audit

Completed 2026-11-08. This is a synthetic archive-level calibration audit with **52,000 independent worlds across all26 frozen cells**, three rules paired within every world, and no real outcomes, real fits, new labels or causal-effect model. The independent checker regenerated every world and recomputed every reported interval and paired contrast. No review concern is marked resolved; real mechanism calibration remains unavailable.

## Main finding and interpretation

Under the baseline exchangeable complete-archive model, joint-max calibration covers the entire eleven-entity menu in **.956** of worlds (95% Wilson MC interval **.9461–.9641**). Entitywise .95 calibration has almost nominal average entity coverage, **.9495** (world-bootstrap interval **.9467–.9524**), but joint menu coverage is only **.5585** (.5366–.5801). Joint-max minus entitywise joint coverage is **.3975** (paired-world bootstrap .3760–.4190). Marginal entity coverage is insufficient for the paper's joint event.

Empirical entitywise Bonferroni is a legitimate comparator and is **uninformative at the baseline sample size**: n_cal=199,M=11 requires order-statistic rank200 in each slot, so every radius is positive infinity. Its coverage1 therefore means an unbounded ball, not superior localization. The first finite rank would require219 independent complete calibration archives. Joint-max needs19 complete archives at eta=.05, provided their maximum-error scores are exchangeable with deployment; nineteen single-study labels do not constitute nineteen complete menus.

Where empirical Bonferroni is finite (K=4,B=0,M=5,n_cal=199), joint-max joint coverage is .9575 (.9477–.9655) and Bonferroni is .9605 (.9510–.9682). Their paired difference, joint-max minus Bonferroni, is -.0030 (-.0105–.0040). The median finite radius is3.0381 for joint-max and3.0906 across Bonferroni slot radii. This cell does not establish a general superiority claim; empirical finite ranks and menu size materially affect feasibility.

## Prespecified stress results, including failures

**Menu size:** with no bridges (M=5), six bridges (M=11 baseline), and twelve bridges (M=17), entitywise joint coverage is .7690, .5585 and .4265, respectively, while average entity coverage stays close to .95. With16 sources and12 bridges (M=29), its joint coverage falls to .2235 (.2058–.2423), versus joint-max .9465 (.9358–.9555). Joint-max median radius increases from3.0381 at M=5 to3.5707 at M=29. All menus were frozen and calibration included every bridge, regardless of whether a future policy might choose it.

**Dimension and dependence:** matched p=8 has joint-max joint coverage .9495 (.9390–.9583), while rho=.6,p=2 has .9455 (.9347–.9546). Within-archive dependence changes the joint/marginal relationship, but independent archive count remains n_cal. At rho=.6 the entitywise joint coverage .7285 is higher than baseline .5585; it still does not meet a .95 joint target. p=8,rho=.6 joint-max coverage is .9490 (.9385–.9578). These are generator-specific stress observations, not a validation of real mechanism dimensions or correlations.

**Calibration sample size:** n_cal=7 or18 gives infinite .95 joint-max and marginal radii in every world. Their coverage1 is vacuous. At n_cal=19, joint-max coverage is .9425 (.9314–.9519); at n_cal=49 it is .9520 (.9417–.9605). Under continuous exchangeable scores the exact unconditional coverage is rank/(n_cal+1), capped at1: .95 at n_cal=19/199 and **.96 at n_cal=49**. The rho=.6,n_cal=49 result .9655 (.9566–.9726) is compared with .96, not mistakenly with an exact .95 finite-sample equality. These MC observations neither prove exchangeability nor certify any real archive.

**Matched descriptor degradation:** with missing-descriptor error added with probability.25 in both calibration and test, joint-max joint coverage is .9500 (.9396–.9587), but median radius rises to8.5180 from3.2869 baseline. At p=8 the corresponding radius is11.2974 and coverage .9525 (.9423–.9610). Matched unreliable descriptors yield coverage .9475 (.9368–.9564) and median radius6.0433; adding rho=.6 yields .9510 (.9406–.9596). Coverage survives in these cells because the degradation distribution was explicitly included in the independent calibration law; informativeness deteriorates. This does not justify arbitrary imputation in real data.

**Deployment shifts:** scaling test error by1.5 without recalibration lowers baseline joint-max joint coverage to .3720 (.3511–.3934); at p=8 it is .0350 (.0278–.0440). Test-only missing-descriptor noise gives .1380 (.1236–.1538) at p=2 and .0030 (.0014–.0065) at p=8. Test-only unreliable descriptors give .3695 (.3486–.3909); with rho=.6 they give .3490 (.3284–.3702). The combined p=8,rho=.6,B=12 missingness shift gives **0/2000** joint coverage (Wilson upper .001917), despite average entity coverage **.5773** (world-bootstrap .5715–.5829). The combined scale-shift cell gives .1205 (.1070–.1355). All failures are retained. Infinite Bonferroni balls still cover every shifted case because they localize nothing; their coverage is not evidence of useful robustness.

## Unit, uncertainty and breakdown

Each world draws an independent calibration sample of complete archives and an independent full-menu test archive. The three rules share those exact draws within the world. Joint coverage is one AND over K sources, B bridges and one target. Entity coverage is a within-world fraction, then averaged over independent worlds. Source/bridge means use the same procedure; B=0 bridge coverage is undefined. Joint and fixed-slot/target intervals use Wilson; aggregate entity/source/bridge fractions and paired method differences use2,000 world bootstrap resamples. All intervals are pointwise MC intervals; multiple cells/roles/methods are not extra independent observations or multiplicity-adjusted evidence.

For the baseline joint-max rule, source coverage is .9965 (.9951–.9978), bridge coverage .9959 (.9947–.9970), and target coverage .9925 (.9877–.9954). Entitywise values are .9514 (.9468–.9561), .9492 (.9452–.9530), and .9445 (.9336–.9537), respectively. Complete per-cell/rule/role results and intervals are in `full01/summary.csv`; each fixed entity slot is in `entity_summary.csv`. All pairings are in `paired_world_contrasts.csv`. A zero empirical paired difference with a degenerate bootstrap interval in a zero-hit cell is not proof of equal population performance; retain the Wilson uncertainty on each joint rate.

The new MC intervals integrate calibration-sample variation because the calibration sample is regenerated in every world. The historical `current_method/results/mechanism_calibration/` audit used one fitted calibrator and2,000 test archives, so its interval is conditional on that particular fitted calibrator. The old `full/joint_calibration` and `full_plus/calibration_sensitivity` experiments already redraw calibration and remain relevant; this incremental grid adds menu/dimension/dependence/descriptor/empirical-Bonferroni comparisons rather than claiming those earlier studies were absent.
